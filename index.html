About

Where the design ends
and the build begins.

I'm a frontend developer and UI/UX designer based in Kattathurai, Kanniyakumari — the southernmost point of mainland India, where the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean meet. It's a strange, useful place to work from: I spend my days at the exact meeting point of two crafts too, translating Figma and Adobe XD files into responsive Angular and WordPress builds that hold up in a real browser.

Over the past three years I've shipped 20+ live client projects spanning corporate sites, WooCommerce stores, custom PHP platforms, and association portals — for teams in India and, increasingly, Bahrain. I care about the seam between design and code: the place most handoffs quietly lose fidelity, and where I try hardest not to.
